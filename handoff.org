** THE STANDING RULE THIS SESSION ADDED
Editing what was read, through the API, is a goal of the crate, for *every*
attribute -- geometry, properties, native, and the collection's own fields --
and no layout change may make an edit worse than the row IR made it. Every
attribute has an owned hatch: an edit as ordinary Rust on the row IR's own
types. This is D-C16; =tests/edits.rs= pins it and must pass unchanged
through every later step.

What was done, in order:
1. *Plan.* D-C16 written, C6 rewritten as seven steps, ACCEPTANCE gained the
   edit criterion, D-C6's stale note fixed (=for_each_position_run_mut= has no
   caller outside its unit tests; =tests/reproject_pipe.rs= uses
   =for_each_position_mut=).
2. *C6 step 1, measure.* Geometry is 73% of the IR on the points bench and
   99% on polygons, so C6 goes on. Inputs are generated by a scratch script
   (polygons of 25--64 vertices, 10% with a hole, 100 000; the points bench
   200 000), not committed -- regenerate them from the description in C6
   step 1 if needed.
3. *C6 step 2, the edit contract* (D-C16), on the old storage:
   =edit_geometry=, =for_each_geometry_mut=, =edit_properties=,
   =edit_feature=, =for_each_feature_mut= on =FeatureCollection=;
   =tests/edits.rs=; C1's edit-sequence property test draws the new edits.
   An edit that leaves a row as it was, bit for bit, is not patched
   (=JsonValue::identical=, =Value::identical=, =table::geometry::identical=:
   floats by their bits, since =-0.0 == 0.0=).
4. *C6 step 3, the flat column* -- =src/table/geometry.rs=. A block's
   =GeometryColumn= is =Owned= (today's vector; =--layout rows= keeps it) or
   =Flat= (tags, row→part→ring→position =u32= offsets, XY buffer, Z/M
   buffers with a presence bitmap once some position lacks one, collections
   in a side table, an overlay of edited rows). Blocks flatten at seal.
   =FeatureRef::geometry()= now returns =GeometryRef= -- the one deliberate
   public break; four tests moved to =to_geometry()=. IR on polygons
   301.9 → 77.3 MB.
5. *C6 step 4, begun.* =GeometryRef::shape()= (=Shape=, =Run=, =Rings=,
   =Parts=) reads either form; WKB (GeoParquet, GeoPackage blob and
   envelope), GeoJSON and FlatGeobuf encode from it.

** NEXT
- *Step 4, the rest.* KML (writer and =reconcile.rs=), WKT
  (=write_wkt_lines=), CSV and Shapefile still copy each geometry out
  through =FeatureRef::geometry_cow= (crate-internal). Move each onto
  =shape()=, as =wkb.rs= / =geojson/mod.rs= / =flatgeobuf/writer.rs= did;
  then =geometry_cow= can go. The Shapefile writer collects every geometry
  into a =Vec<Option<Geometry>>= first (it did before C6 too), so it is the
  one writer whose peak still holds an owned copy of the whole collection.
- *Step 5, hot readers write coordinates.* The remaining time cost: every
  conversion is 0--9% slower than the C5 pin, =parquet→fgb= on polygons 17%,
  because a block builds owned geometry and then flattens it at seal. GeoJSON,
  WKB, FlatGeobuf and Shapefile readers should push into =Flat= directly
  (=Flat::push= is the seam; =RowBuilder=, =TabularBlock= and
  =Table::from_columns= hold =Vec<Option<Geometry>>= until seal today).
- *Step 7, verify.* Lower the budgets in =tests/budgets.rs= to what C6
  measures; =compare.py= within noise of C5 (needs =ogr2ogr=, =duckdb=,
  =terrio=, =geoscribe= on =PATH=).
- Step 6 (edits) is mostly in already: single-row edits on a flat block go
  through the overlay; bulk passes make a block owned and flat again; the
  position editors walk the buffers and grow Z/M where an edit adds one.
  Nothing folds the geometry overlay except a bulk pass, as with properties.
- Not decided: =GeometryRef='s public reading surface beyond =to_geometry=,
  =type_name=, =bbox=, =extend_bbox=, =has_m=. =Shape= and friends are
  crate-internal; making them public would let a dependent read coordinates
  without a copy.
